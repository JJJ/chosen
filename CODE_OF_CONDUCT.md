# Code of Conduct

Chosen welcomes contributions and questions from people of different backgrounds
and experience levels. Please help keep its issues, discussions, pull requests,
and other project spaces useful and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and constructive, especially when you disagree.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Respect different skill levels, identities, and viewpoints.
- Keep public discussions relevant to the project and avoid sharing another
  person's private information.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual attention, intimidation,
and repeated disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or hide
content, lock conversations, or restrict participation when needed to protect
the community. They will consider context and severity when taking action.

## Reporting

If you see a conduct problem on GitHub, use the **Report content** option on
the relevant comment, issue, discussion, or account. GitHub handles those
reports under its [Community Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines).
For an immediate threat, contact local emergency services.

Maintainers can address behavior in Chosen's project spaces, but cannot promise
confidentiality for reports made in public threads. Please do not post personal
details or allegations publicly.
